# Month 1 Summary

## Question
How accessible are health facilities within my study area?

## Spatial Operation
I used the Buffer tool to create a 500 meters buffer around health facilities. I used a projected CRS with metres as the unit to ensure the buffer distance was accurate.

## What I Expected and What I Got
I expected the 500 meters buffer to create an accessibility zone around each health facility. The result covered all areas of 500 meters of the health facilities.

## What Surprised Me
I observed that all areas within the study area were covered by the 500 meters health facility buffers, indicating that they are not farther than 500 meters from the mapped health facilities.

## What Data I Still Need
I still need population data and complete health facility information to better assess healthcare accessibility across the study area.
